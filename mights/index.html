About the project

A world built around
the Moodys.

Moody Mights brings a distinctive cast of collectible characters into an evolving Web3 gaming ecosystem. The original Moody Mights collection lives on Ethereum, while Moody Madness expands the characters into playable experiences on Abstract, including an arcade racing game, Moody Drops, The Vault and collectible systems.
